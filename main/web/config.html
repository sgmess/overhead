Radar

Tap an empty part of the scope to step through the ranges.

Altitude is in hundreds of feet with a climb or descent arrow; speed is ground speed in knots.

Where each of the nearest 50 aircraft has been, fading with age.

Military aircraft get a pink diamond. Routes come from adsb.im, which is sent the callsign of the aircraft you tap; a question mark means the route doesn't fit where it is. Helicopters are always drawn with a rotor.

Overhead alert

The aircraft blinks amber and a banner names it; tap the banner for its card. The screen comes up to full brightness at night while it lasts. Altitude is pressure altitude, so near an airport set the ceiling with that in mind.

Map layers

Airspace and airfields from OpenAIP, for 107 NM around the centre, refreshed daily. They need a free OpenAIP account and API key, created on openaip.net under your profile's API clients.

OpenAIP data is fetched a country at a time. Left empty, it's the country of the airfield nearest the centre; near a border, list the neighbours as well, e.g. FR,CH.
